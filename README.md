## On Change

I’m not trying to change the world. I’m not even trying to change Cascade. In a lot of ways, I don’t want Cascade to change.

What I will fight against is anyone saying we can’t change or shouldn’t change.

Change is inevitable. Sometimes it hurts. Sometimes it’s terrifying. Sometimes you would give anything to stop it.

But change is also one of the best parts of being alive.

Some of the greatest experiences of my life only happened because something changed—sometimes because I chose it, and sometimes because life chose it for me.

I love the life I have and the place I live. But I refuse to become so afraid of losing what I have that I close the door on whatever incredible thing might come next.

I’m not willing to risk never having the best parts of my life again just because I was afraid of change.
