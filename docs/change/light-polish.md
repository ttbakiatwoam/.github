# Change — Light Polish

I’m not trying to change the world. I’m not even trying to change Cascade. In a lot of ways, I don’t want Cascade to change.

But I will fight to my last breath against anyone who says we cannot change, or that we should not change.

Because change is inevitable. And honestly, I think it’s one of the best parts of being alive—one of the best parts of being human.

That doesn’t mean change doesn’t hurt. It doesn’t mean it isn’t sometimes painful, frightening, or difficult.

But in my relatively short time on this planet, some of the best experiences of my life have happened because something changed. Because I changed. Because my circumstances changed. Because life took me somewhere I never expected to go.

And I’m not willing to spend the rest of my life trying so hard to preserve what I have that I risk never experiencing the best parts of my life again.
