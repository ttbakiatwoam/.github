# Change — Original Dictation

_Dictated by Andrew Saunders on September 10, 2026. Preserved verbatim and unedited._

I’m not trying to change the world. I’m not even trying to change Cascade in some ways. I don’t even want Cascade to change, but I will fight to my last breath against anyone who says we cannot change and we should not change because change is inevitable and it’s the best part of being alive and being a human that doesn’t mean it doesn’t hurt that doesn’t mean it’s sometimes not painful. But in my short time on this planet best experience of my life have always come because of change and I’m not gonna risk never having the best parts of my life again.
