# Change — More Lyrical

I’m not trying to change the world. I’m not even trying to change Cascade. In a lot of ways, I hope Cascade never loses the things that make it Cascade.

But I will fight to my last breath against the idea that we cannot change, or that we should be afraid to.

Change is inevitable. It is uncomfortable. Sometimes it hurts like hell. Sometimes it takes things from us we desperately wanted to keep.

But change is also one of the greatest parts of being alive. It is one of the things that makes us human.

In my short time on this planet, the best experiences of my life have almost always existed on the other side of change. Places I never expected to go. People I never expected to meet. Lives I never imagined I would live.

I don’t want to lose the things I love.

But I’m even more afraid of holding onto them so tightly that I never discover the next best part of my life.
